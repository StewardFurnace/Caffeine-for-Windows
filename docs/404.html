What changes when the cup is on

Caffeine asks Windows to stay awake the same way a video player does. Your power settings stay exactly as they are.

Without Caffeine With Caffeine on

Sleep

The PC sleeps after a few idle minutes and long downloads, backups or renders stop

The PC stays awake until you turn Caffeine off or the timer ends

Screen

The display turns off while you read, present or watch a dashboard

The display stays on (or turn that off and keep only the PC awake)

Lock screen

Windows locks after inactivity and you type your PIN again and again

With “Prevent auto-lock” on, Windows sees you as active and doesn’t lock

Teams and Slack

Your status turns yellow “Away” after a few minutes without typing

You stay “Available” while Caffeine is on

Everything is in one small tray menu

No window to manage and nothing running in the background except the cup next to your clock.

Stop sleep, screen off and auto-lock with one click

Left-click toggles Caffeine. Right-click opens the menu with the timer and a few options you set once.

  • Keep the display on, or keep only the PC awake with the screen allowed to turn off
  • Prevent auto-lock and the “Away” status in Teams, Slack and other chat apps
  • Start with Windows and switch on automatically

Keep awake for an hour, then sleep as usual

Starting a long download before bed? Pick Keep awake for 2 hours. The tooltip on the cup shows the time left.

When the timer ends, Caffeine turns itself off and Windows goes back to your normal sleep settings. Nothing to remember.

Doesn’t touch your power plan

Caffeine uses SetThreadExecutionState, the Windows API made for this. It doesn’t edit power settings, so there is nothing to undo later.

Questions before you download

Can’t find your answer? Open an issue on GitHub.

How do I keep my PC from going to sleep without changing power settings?

Run Caffeine for Windows and click the coffee cup next to the clock. While the cup is orange, Windows won’t sleep or turn off the screen. Your power plan isn’t changed, so closing Caffeine brings back your usual settings instantly.

Does Caffeine work on Windows 11?

Yes. It works on Windows 10 and Windows 11, including the latest updates, because it uses the documented SetThreadExecutionState API rather than tricks that break between Windows versions.

How do I stop Teams from showing me as Away?

Right-click the cup and turn on Prevent auto-lock and “Away” status. After 59 seconds without input, Caffeine presses F15, a key no app uses, so Teams, Slack and Windows see you as active. It never moves your mouse or types anything.

Is it safe? Does it need admin rights?

It’s a small open source program with no installer, no network access and no admin rights. It only stores its options under your user account in the registry. Follow your company’s rules before turning off the lock screen on a work PC.

Can I keep the PC awake but let the screen turn off?

Yes. Untick Keep display on in the menu. The screen then turns off as usual, while downloads, backups and remote sessions keep running.

Is this the Caffeine app by Zhorn Software?

No. Caffeine for Windows is an independent open source project. It is not affiliated with Zhorn Software, and it’s built for Windows 10 and 11 from the ground up.
